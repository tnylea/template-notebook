Coda began over coffee in 2022. Jordan Tate and I had each been writing scattered things on the internet for a decade. The idea was to bring four essays a quarter into one place, print it on paper, and mail it to anyone who cared enough to ask.

Sixteen issues in, the formula has barely changed: four essays, one interview, a recommendation page, and a fold-out at the back. The format constrains everything else — the editorial, the production, the price.

We don't run ads. We don't sell the list. We make a small profit per issue and reinvest it in better paper. It is, by every contemporary metric, a foolish business. We love it.
