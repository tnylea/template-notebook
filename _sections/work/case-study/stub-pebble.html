Pebble OS — a desktop environment that respects attention.

A six-month exploration into a calmer desktop. No notifications. No badges. No unread counts. A complete operating system designed around the idea that you decide when to be interrupted.

Concept · Public

Pebble OS is the project I made for myself in 2023, mostly to think out loud about what an operating system could be if attention were the most expensive thing on it.

Most operating systems treat your focus as something to be siphoned by every app that thinks it deserves a slice. Pebble inverts that contract: nothing reaches you unless you've explicitly subscribed to it, and your subscription expires every 24 hours.

The project is unfinished and probably will remain so. It exists as a static-image concept and a small interactive prototype. I get an email about it every other month. I'm always glad to receive them.
