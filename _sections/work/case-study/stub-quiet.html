Quiet Radio — ambient music for the background of life.

A two-year side project: a small streaming app of curated ambient music. Six channels. No algorithm. No feed. It found its people, then quietly retired.

Type

Lifespan

Subscribers

Status
Sunset, gracefully

Quiet Radio was a small thing I made for myself and a few hundred other people who, like me, just wanted music in the room.

Six hand-curated channels — Morning, Studio, Evening, Stillness, Walking, Sleep — each with a small, slow rotation of tracks I genuinely loved. No discovery algorithm. No suggested-for-you. The point was the curation, and the curation was me.

I sunset it in 2023, after two years. It was profitable but not growing, and it was slowly eating the time I wanted to spend on bigger work. I miss it sometimes. I do not regret stopping.
