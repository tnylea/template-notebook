Folio — a quieter reading app, designed for one tab open at a time.

Concept and product design for a longform reader that earns its place on your home screen. Three months, two designers, one extremely opinionated mood-board.

Client
Folio Readers
Role
Lead designer
Scope
Product, brand
Status
Shipped

Folio came out of a single, slightly cranky observation: most reading apps are optimized for the moment of acquiring something to read, not the act of reading it.

We spent three months on the question "what does a reading app look like if reading is the actual goal?" The answer, mostly, was less of everything.

We removed the social feed. We removed streaks. We removed the algorithmic recommendations. We added the only feature anyone has ever asked us for after using it: a small, unobtrusive line at the bottom of every article that says "You started this 23 minutes ago. Want to keep going?"

The app launched in March 2025 to a quiet, devoted audience. It is small and patient and currently unprofitable. We are mostly fine with that.
