How I Work

Four things I keep coming back to.

Less a manifesto than a set of biases. They show up in the work whether I want them to or not — these days I mostly want them to.

№01

Patience over speed.

A second week with a project usually beats a second feature. Most of what looks like polish is actually time.

№02

Smaller is mostly better.

I'd rather make one excellent thing for a hundred people than one okay thing for ten thousand. The math works out, eventually.

№03

Type does most of the work.

Choose the typeface, set the body well, get the headline rhythm right — and most "design problems" quietly stop being problems.

№04

The work is a relationship.

Best results come from clients who feel like collaborators. I take fewer projects and stay on each one longer than is fashionable.
