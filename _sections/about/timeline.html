Year by Year

A working timeline.

A short, honest version. The good years and the boring ones, in order.

  1. 2026 · now

    Studio of one — and counting.

    Working with three patient clients this year, shipping Acre Type's second release, and finishing the manuscript for a small book on craft.

  2. 2024

    Coda №14 — and a real readership.

    The quarterly letter crossed 3,000 paid subscribers. We added a paper edition. Spoke at Config and OFFF. Taught a workshop in Lisbon I'm still thinking about.

  3. 2022

    Left Field & Form. Started for myself.

    Spent the first six months mostly walking. Co-founded Coda with Jordan. Took my first solo client. Was scared the entire time and would do it again immediately.

  4. 2018

    Senior designer at Field & Form.

    Led design on a long, unglamorous, deeply-loved B2B reading product. Learned more from that project than any portfolio could possibly suggest.

  5. 2015

    First "real" design job.

    Junior designer at a small studio whose name I still mention quietly. Twenty-three, anxious, lucky in a way I didn't yet understand.

  6. 2014 · before

    Studied writingsism. Stayed for the layouts.

    Made a zine. Got obsessed with InDesign. Read too many books on grids. Quietly began suspecting I was in the wrong major.
